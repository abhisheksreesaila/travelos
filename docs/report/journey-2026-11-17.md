# The journey, walked as a critic (2026-11-17)

## The soul
GitAway is the friend who has already been there. You say a wish in plain words, like "Tahoe with the kids, around Christmas". It answers with the whole trip, flights and stay together, across nearby places and nearby days, priced as one number. You can see at a glance which week and which town is the smart one. Then it tells you the truth the listings leave out: the resort fee, the parking, the runway next door, the beach that needs a car, what guests actually complain about. You decide in one sitting, book it in one place, and the trip becomes a plan your family shares, and later a story someone else can copy.

Three promises carry it. Every page is judged against them:
1. **The whole trip, one number.** Never make me add a flight to a hotel in my head.
2. **The truth, up front.** What others hide, we show proudly, and we never invent it.
3. **Calm confidence.** A deep, quiet ground, one bold action per screen, nothing that makes me wait without reason.
