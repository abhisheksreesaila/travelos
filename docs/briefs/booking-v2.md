# Booking, world class (v2)

Status: approved (founder, 2026-11-14 night, in their words below; replaces the journey brief's steps 3 to 5)

## In the founder's words (condensed, nothing added)
- **Sentence as a query, not a text box.** "I cannot type text." The landing is Google-plain: one sentence whose every part is a tappable word (where, when, how many nights, who, budget, what you're into). You help frame it; you change any word in place.
- **Search becomes the results, on the same page.** Like Google: the sentence moves up, a skeleton animation plays while it loads, then the matrix fills in like Google Flights: well defined, well spaced, the results of flight + hotel and the total, the chosen nights shown clearly, recommended vs not marked. Change any word → skeleton again → the matrix reloads.
- **The matrix is centre stage and loads like a map**: scroll to later dates or down to other places and it loads more. Weather woven into it, from the real weather APIs.
- **Translucent widgets beside it, like Apple's**: weather; a map (how far the hotel is from where I want to be, from the airport, from me); Uber cost; live or predicted traffic; a cheaper airport nearby; food that fits me (vegetarian, Indian, free breakfast); what's trending, news, top 10 places. At my fingertips before I click a square. Keep the gradient background behind the bar.
- **Click a square → a drawer flows down from the matrix**: hotel and flight side by side; the hotel's gallery and rooms to choose from; looking at the hotel minimises the flight and vice versa (liquid motion). Change hotel or flight there.
- **Book my trip** → it spins → the typical questions → review → submit → done → "Plan this trip".
- **Plan** (after booking is polished): a simple calendar of events with the flight and hotel already in; add anything easily; a side panel to plan with others; weather and extra info there too. No map needed.
- **Type**: "the fonts making up the application… it's crap." The cover's title is good. Find the best practice; world class.
