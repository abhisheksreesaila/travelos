# Guides, and a calmer palette (v2.1)

Status: approved direction (founder, 2026-11-15, walking the v2 build: "the navigation, the micro-interaction is absolutely brilliant … this is the gold standard … truly liquid")

## In the founder's words (condensed, nothing added)
- **Too Instagram-ish:** "the pink and the violet, it's sprayed everywhere … only the Instagrammable page or card should have that theme. Everything else, pick your own theme … reduce that violet-pink effect on those buttons."
- **Navigation:** "I see Book, I see Plan, but Family leads to an outdated page … the group you add is your family; the collaboration is in the drawer … remove Family and make it creators: you book, you plan, you create. We need a better word for creators." And "there has to be a quick access point for community: I want to see what people have done."
- **Community in context:** "I might see it before, while or after … when I book, it has to be next to it so I can decide; when I plan, next to it … without losing the context of booking. Discovery (the cover) is the easy part."
- **No book for now:** "creators just put their thoughts in sequence, add stuff and their affiliate links … people want to scroll quickly … more than a blog but less than a book … the nitty-gritty details laid out so I grasp the information quickly, and click to get the details: a master-detail view, a widgets view, a scroll or timeline view … you'll have to invent this … go read a San Diego travel blog and see what information I'd want."
- **Keep:** the micro-interactions and liquid motion are "the gold standard"; the founder wants to borrow them for other sites once this prototype is finished.

## Calls (mine, to be judged in the prototype)
- The word is **Guides**: you make a guide from your trip or your post; others' guides are where community lives. Nav: **Book · Plan · Guides**; the avatar menu keeps account things.
- Palette: ink and indigo carry the UI; kind colours only for kinds; the pink-violet gradient only on the Instagram share card.
