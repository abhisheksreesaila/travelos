# Planning, world class (v2)

Status: approved direction (founder, 2026-11-14; to be prototyped like booking v2, in its design language)

## In the founder's words (condensed, nothing added)
- After booking, "Plan this trip": a simple calendar of events with the flight and hotel already loaded. "No map is required; I just need a calendar of events." A simple calendar view is fine; day and week views; "a very themed version of a Microsoft Teams calendar."
- Put in whatever I like, very easily: click to add (finger later).
- A side panel to collaborate: people plan it together, contribute, comment.
- Help me plan, not just an empty grid: links fitted to what I said I like (vegetarian → vegetarian restaurants; seafood → seafood places), the top places other GitAway travellers went, existing plans others made, weather nudges ("rain Thursday: take an umbrella"). Pictures nicely arranged. Then save.
- Weather widgets and extra info there too, "available for it to be useful, no pressure".
- Same feel as booking v2: the gradient, the translucent cards, Newsreader with Inter.
