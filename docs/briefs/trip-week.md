# Brief: the trip week

Status: approved (captain, 2026-10-02, answers in chat: hide everything not real; morning plan first; family see it in the app)

## Why
The captain leaves on a 7-day family trip (Oct 4–10, 2026). For that week GitAway must be the family's one place for the plan: clean, real, on iPhones. Booking, community and creators wait until after the trip.

## What
1. **A clean live app.** The live site shows only real things: no sample trips, no Community trips page or section, no creators pages, no fake fork counts or names. The landing keeps its story (Plan a trip, the four features, the animated calendar as an illustration). The booking workspace stays, clearly a preview with sample prices. The local copy keeps all sample data for review.
2. **Today, laid out to read and share.** Invited family already see the same live plan. A clean "Today" view of the day's plan, easy to read on a phone, plus a Share button that sends a short text summary of the day (Messages/WhatsApp) for anyone not in the app.
3. **Morning plan push.** Each morning at a time the person picks, a push notification with today's plan that opens the Today view. Works on iPhone once GitAway is on the Home Screen.
4. Smaller: sign-out is instant; invites are shared links (no email sender yet).

## Not now
Leave-by and booking reminders, location while open, email invites (needs an email provider or the captain's domain), Face ID, real booking, community, creators.

## How we'll know
The captain uses it all week: edits the plan daily, gets the morning push on their iPhone, and the family follows the plan in the app.
