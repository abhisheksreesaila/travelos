# The trip canvas (F-081)

One surface for the trip that zooms: week, day, block (a park day with its parts and steps), step. Module `gitaway/pages/tripcanvas.py`, styles `assets/css/trip_canvas.css`, script `assets/js/trip_canvas.js`. Designs: frames 5, 6, 7 and 11 of `docs/design/canvas/Trip-Canvas-v2.html`, frames 1 to 3 of `Plan-Steps-v1.html`.

## Addresses (every level has one, drawn by the server)

| Level | Address | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Week | `/trip/canvas` | a row per day; park days show their parts as mini blocks with counts and one note sticker; free days have a `+` (editors) |
| Day | `/trip/canvas?day=N` | blocks in time order; a park block shows its parts with steps as chips, notes as stickers on their item, who as avatars; the day's Set aside tray |
| Block | `/trip/canvas?block=aN` | parts as sections, steps in order, lanes where people split up, the block's Set aside tray, the trip's lists |
| Step | `/trip/canvas?step=<id>` | a bottom sheet over its block: note, who is going, Mark done / Not done, Set aside / Put back |

An unknown address goes back to the week. `?frag=1` returns the level alone (no page around it) for the script. `POST /trip/canvas/step` does the four writes (`gitaway.canvas.set_done`, `set_aside`) and, for the script (`X-Canvas: 1`), answers 204 with `X-Canvas-Url`; without script it redirects. Only a `/trip/canvas…` address is accepted as the way back. Writes are gated by `gitaway.access` like every write: a viewer reads every level and sees no buttons and no `+`.

## Where it is reached from

Today (`/trip`) stays the first tab. Its heading has a "Week view" link (`#tp-open-canvas`, every role), and the canvas has a Today | Week | Day control (`Today` leaves the canvas). The phone tab bar is unchanged (the canvas shows Today as the current tab). Replacing Today with the canvas, as the storyboard draws it, is a later decision for the captain once the family has used both.

## The zoom

A tap on a link marked `data-zoom` ("in", "out", "side"), a two-finger pinch, or the browser's Back fetches the level as a fragment and swaps it inside `document.startViewTransition`. The tapped element (`data-zk="day-2" | "blk-a3" | "stp-<id>"`) is named `cz-hero` before the swap and its twin after it, so a day row grows into the day heading, a block card into the block heading, a step chip into the sheet, and back. The page behind scales and fades with a soft blur (`html[data-cz-dir]`), the tab bar stays put. Durations and easing are `--cz-dur` and `--cz-ease` on `:root`.

- No View Transitions: the new level scales and fades in (`.cz-in-in`, `.cz-in-out`, `.cz-in-side`).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: an instant swap, no transition at all.
- Pinch: removed (F-092, it did not work reliably on the captain's iPhone). The Day | Week toggle (`#cz-z-day`, `#cz-z-week`), every row and the back button zoom; Escape zooms out. The canvas sets `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom` (sideways moves are the flick; the page's own pinch zoom works).
- Zooming out to where you came from is the browser's Back (history stays tidy); a write from the sheet zooms out to its block.
- Mark done and Set aside write one coalesced family card ("Abhi finished 3 rides at Universal Studios Hollywood", changed in place for 15 minutes, one push) in `gitaway.canvas._tell`.

## Lanes

Steps of a part that share a time and belong to different people (`who`, with Everyone being nobody in particular) are drawn side by side, one lane per set of people, labelled with the avatars; everything else runs full width (`pages.tripcanvas.slots`).

## Wide day view

At 721px and up the same markup is the laptop view: the week as a strip of day cards across the top (a bar segment per part), the day's parts as lanes (name on the left, rides beside it), the Set aside tray at the side. A strip card swaps the day with a plain cross-fade.

## Touch moves and filters (F-082)

Frame 8 of `Trip-Canvas-v2.html` and frame 4 of `Plan-Steps-v1.html`. Editors only (a viewer has no `data-edit` on `#cz`: no drag, no swipe, no add, no Move; the filters still work).

**Hold and drag** (`trip_canvas.js`, pointer events). Hold a step (a day chip, a block row, a Set aside chip) for 350 ms: it dims, a chip follows the finger, and the drop targets light up. Moving more than 10px first is a scroll. Targets: a part (a line shows where it goes; before the step the finger is above, else at the end), a day in the bar that appears at the top (blocks on other days), the Set aside tray along the bottom. Letting go posts `POST /trip/canvas/move` and swaps the level in place (`dir: side`, scroll kept), then the toast ("<title> moved to <where>") with Undo. A step dropped before a timed step takes that step's time (so it can join or leave a lane); at the end of a part it keeps its own. Dropped on another day it lands at the end of that block's same-named part, else its first. Dragging a Set aside chip onto a part puts it back there.

**Undo** posts the snapshot the move returned (`canvas.move_step` -> `undo`) to `POST /trip/canvas/undo`; `canvas.restore` checks every id, part, time and number in it against the open trip and puts those steps back exactly (block, part, place, time, tray). The family card ("Abhi moved 3 rides at Universal", `canvas._tell` column `moved`) is changed in place for 15 minutes and loses the move when it is undone; adds share the same mechanism ("added").

**Swipe.** On a block row, left reveals Done (Not done) and Set aside (the row gives up room instead of sliding off), right or a tap elsewhere puts them away; they are the same forms as the sheet's. **Add a step** is a sheet over the block, `?block=aN&add=1[&part=&title=&note=]` (works without script): a name, who (Everyone is one choice, then each member, names and initials the trip already uses, Adults, Kids), which part, an optional time (the GitAway time picker), a note; `POST /trip/canvas/add`. **Add a note** and **Move** are on the step sheet: the note form (`POST /trip/canvas/note`, the sheet stays) and a Move menu with the same targets as dragging as buttons (earlier or later in the part, each other part, each other day), for a keyboard or a screen reader.

**Filters.** On a day and a block: Everyone, each family member, names and initials the trip uses, Adults and Kids (only when a step is for them: the family's ages are not known), and each named list of the trip. Single choice; the matching steps get a ring, the rest dim. A step for everyone matches every person. A list matches steps by name (`canvas.same_step`: equal, or one starts with the other by whole words, so "Soarin'" is "Soarin' Around the World"). With a list chosen, a card says "4 more from your <list> list aren't in this day yet" with a chip for each: tap to open the add sheet filled in, or drag it onto a part to add it there. The choice is kept per person and trip in `localStorage` (`cz-filter:<trip>:<user>`; it still works for the page if storage is blocked).

**Writes** (`/trip/canvas/step|move|undo|add|note`) are editor-gated by `gitaway.access`, take the trip the page was drawn for (`trip` field), and the script gets JSON `{url, toast, undo}` or 422 `{error}`.

## The plan of the day (F-090)

## The day as a time grid; move and resize by touch (F-097)

Brief `docs/briefs/touch-day.md` (the captain: "blocks of time where I can edit with my finger, just like I would edit a Teams meeting"). Code: `tripcanvas.py` (`time_grid`, `grid_block`, `grid_bookings`, `grid_range`, `plan_write`, `POST /trip/canvas/plan`), `gitaway/planedit.py`, `assets/js/day_grid.js`, `assets/css/day_grid.css`.

**The grid.** A day with a plan draws the plans as blocks on an hour grid (7 AM to 10 PM, wider for an earlier or later booking or plan): the block's place and height are its time (`--s` minutes after the grid's first hour, `--l` minutes long, 1 minute = `--mm`; 4.5rem an hour on a phone, 5.5rem on a laptop). Overlaps sit side by side (`calendar.lanes`). Bookings are the quiet dashed lines of F-090/F-093 at their times (`.cz-gbk`, a tap opens the booking's sheet; a line that would sit on another slides below it, its label keeps the true time) and the plans leave a narrow strip free so a booking's icon still shows under them. Today has a now line (`#cz-nowline`, the script moves it). A park block (a plan with parts and steps) is a block too: it moves and resizes as a whole, its parts are small labels (they stay drop targets for a list chip or a set-aside step), a tap opens its block level; its steps are picked up in the block level as before (F-082). A blank day keeps Talk / Paste and its booking lines. The week, the Day | Week toggle, the kind and who/list filters (a block lights when any of its steps match), SOS and the flick between days are unchanged; a laptop gets the same markup wider. A viewer's page has no `data-edit`: no grip, no menu button. A tap on a plan opens its chat, for everyone (the captain's call); a tap on a park block opens its block level. Every block is at least 2.75rem tall (a 15-minute plan is drawn taller than its time, its words say the true time), a short block fades what does not fit and shows all of itself when focused, held or with its menu. An Undo carries the values the edit left (`undo.after`) and is refused ("Someone changed it since") when another member changed the plan; times and lengths nobody changed are never re-snapped or held to the forms' 30-minute minimum (`tripcal._clean(was=)`), so phone plans stay editable on the desktop and by Ask. Writes time out after 12 s and a failed save puts the block back.

**Gestures** (`day_grid.js`, editors). Hold a block about 350 ms: it lifts (scale and shadow, a light buzz where the browser has `navigator.vibrate`, which iPhone Safari does not), a dashed ghost shows the 15-minute slot it will land in, a label under the finger shows the new time, and the page scrolls when the finger is near the top or the bottom (above the tab bar). Hold the bottom edge instead: the grid zooms in around the finger, about 2.5x, on one layer with a `scaleY` whose origin is the edge being dragged (words and hairlines scale back by `1/--zk`, so they keep their size), 5-minute steps while zoomed, and eases back when the finger lifts; the shortest plan is 15 minutes. Letting go posts `op=edit` with the new `start` and `end` and the block lands in its place (a FLIP), then a quiet refresh makes the page what the server says. Nothing is read from layout inside a frame: the geometry is read once at the lift, then each frame writes a `transform` (move), one block's height (resize) and the zoom, from the latest pointer. Vertical scroll stays native until a hold lifts a block (`touch-action: pan-y`, and the first touchmove after the hold is cancelled); a sideways flick on empty grid, or one that starts on a block before the hold, still changes the day; a held block never flicks (`CZ.held`). Two fingers are never a gesture. Reduced motion: no zoom, no easing, 15-minute steps.

**Saving.** `POST /trip/canvas/plan`, `op` = `edit` (any of `start`, `end` as minutes, `title`), `undo` (the snapshot an edit returned, or `{"deleted": id}`) or `delete`; the script gets JSON `{toast, undo, plan}`, a refusal is 422 `{error}` in the calendar's own words, a viewer is refused by `gitaway.access`. `planedit.change` goes through `tripcal.update_in` with `fine=True`: times snap to 5 minutes and a plan may be 15 minutes long (the forms keep 15 and 30); overlapping is never refused (F-086). The family is told once: the first move writes a card, and the same person's later nudges of that plan inside `canvas.CARD_WINDOW` change that card in place with no second push; an Undo back to where the plan began takes the card away (`planedit._tell`). A plan Ask adds with a start and no length is an hour (`say._hour_long`), and nobody is asked how long.

Tests: `tests/test_planedit.py` (the model), `tests/test_day_grid.py` (the grid and the write over HTTP), `tests_browser/test_day_grid.py` (390 and 320: sizes and lanes, move, resize with the zoom, Undo, edge scroll, a viewer, reduced motion, flick against hold, a real finger, phone checks). ## Hold menu, rename in place, delete (F-098)

Code: `assets/js/day_menu.js` (loads after `day_grid.js`, which hands it `CZ.hold`, `CZ.tap`, `CZ.setTimes`, `CZ.undo`), styles at the end of `day_grid.css`, the write is `POST /trip/canvas/plan` (`op=edit` with `title`, `op=delete`, `op=undo`).

**The hold menu.** Hold a block (or its bottom edge) and let go without moving: it is not moved, a "Fine: 5 min" switch in the menu makes the four moves 5 minutes (precision without the zoom), it wiggles (`rotate`, nothing under reduced motion, where a ring still marks it) and a small card pops under it (over it when there is no room above the tab bar): Chat (a real link to the plan's chat, `data-talk`), Rename, Delete, and Earlier, Later, Shorter, Longer by 15 minutes (the same writes as dragging, so a keyboard and a screen reader have every gesture; a nudge that would leave the grid or go under 15 minutes is disabled). It is `role="menu"` with `role="menuitem"` buttons of at least 44px. A finger that held lands on the menu itself; a keyboard or a screen reader (`.cz-gb-menubtn`, a visually hidden "Change <title>" button on every block, or Enter on a focused plan) lands on the first item, arrows, Home and End move, Escape closes it and gives the focus back to the block (the canvas's own Escape, which zooms out a level, is not reached). A nudge keeps the menu open on the same item: it is put back on the refreshed block. Tapping away puts it away and opens nothing.

**Delete** asks once, in the menu ("Delete Lunch?", and for a park day "Its 16 steps go with it"; Keep it or Delete), then the block fades, and the toast says "Lunch deleted" (or "... and its 16 steps deleted") with Undo (`planedit.undelete`: the plan and its steps are back exactly).

**Rename in place.** Two quick taps on a title, or Rename in the menu: the title becomes a field right there (16px, so a phone does not zoom in; a short block grows while it is edited). Enter or tapping away saves ("Renamed to ..." with Undo), Escape cancels. An empty title is refused with "Give it a title." and one over 40 characters with "Keep the title to 40 characters." (the calendar's own rules): Enter keeps the field open with the reason under it; tapping away from a title that cannot be saved puts the old one back and says why in the toast. A park block's single tap on its title waits a third of a second for a second tap before it opens; every other tap opens at once.

Tests: `tests_browser/test_day_menu.py` (390 and 320: the menu, every item pressed, Delete with Keep and Undo, a park day's steps, the four nudges and their limits, tapping away, the keyboard, double tap, Enter, Escape, tapping away, the refusals, a viewer, reduced motion), `tests/test_day_grid.py` (the attributes). Screenshots: `F097_SHOTS=<folder> pixi run pytest -p no:randomly tests_browser/test_day_menu.py tests_browser/test_day_grid.py`.

## Make a plan by touching empty time (F-101)

Code: `assets/js/day_new.js` (loads last), the shared title field `CZ.titleField` in `day_menu.js` (Rename uses it too), `planedit.add` / `planedit.unadd`, `POST /trip/canvas/plan` `op=add` (`day`, `start`, `end` in minutes, `title`; Undo is `{"added": id}`), styles `.cz-gb.is-new`, `.cz-gnew` at the end of `day_grid.css`.

Hold empty time of the day grid still for about 350 ms (a scroll, the flick between days, a second finger, a block or a booking line never start it): a one-hour block appears at the 15-minute slot under the finger and follows it; on release its title field opens in place with the keyboard up. Enter or tapping away saves (the calendar's own add rules with the grid's 5-minute grain, overlaps allowed), with a toast and Undo; Escape or an empty title removes it, a title the calendar refuses stays open with the reason (tapping away from it gives it up and says why). A tap on empty time shows a "+" in its slot (gone after 6 s or another tap); pressing it does the same. Keyboard and screen reader: a visually hidden "Add a plan" button first in the grid. Once saved it is a plan like any other (hold to move, hold the bottom edge to resize).

**The iOS rule.** Safari opens the keyboard only for `focus()` inside a user activation (touchend, pointerup, click), not from a timer. The 350 ms timer therefore only shows the block; `openField` runs from the same `pointerup` that ends the hold (or from the click on the "+"), and retries once on the touchend and click that follow if the focus was refused. A blur in the first 350 ms after opening (the mouse events a touch ends with) is taken back, not treated as tapping away; a real tap away (the next `pointerdown`, even on something that is not a control, where iOS would not blur) saves.

While the title is typed the grid is calm (`.is-making`: other blocks and bookings dimmed and untouchable, no gesture, no flick; `CZ.editing()` keeps the revalidation from swapping the page). The family is told once: `planedit.add` tags its "added" card so a move, resize or rename of the new plan inside `canvas.CARD_WINDOW` rewrites that card (still "added", at the new time, no second push); Undo of the add removes the plan and, while it is the newest card, the card.

Stale refreshes: `trip_canvas.js` counts writes (`epoch`); a revalidation or a quiet refresh that was fetched before a write and answers after it is dropped (it redrew the block as it was before the previous hold-menu nudge, so Shorter applied twice). A dropped quiet refresh is asked for again, so a failed later write cannot leave the screen stale.

Review fixes. **A blank day:** an editor gets the grid there too, under one compact Talk / Paste row (`say_bar_compact`, same ids); a viewer keeps the plain "Nothing planned yet" view and the booking lines. **Undo of an add** carries `after` (what it was made as) and is refused ("Someone changed it since. Nothing was undone.") when the plan differs. **One transaction:** `tripcal.add_activity(fine=True)` writes the family card with its tag (`key`, `orig`) itself. **Retries:** the grid carries `data-next` (the calendar's next number) and the script names each new plan `a<n>`, so Enter pressed again after a lost reply, or a retry, never makes it twice. The hidden "Add a plan" button shows when the keyboard reaches it. A blur in the first 350 ms after the field opens leaves it open and is never refocused from the handler (iOS refuses that); a tap on the field brings the keyboard back. The browser tests' server now shuts down with a 2 s grace so idle fetches left open at the end of a session do not fail teardown.

Tests: `tests/test_planedit.py` (add, card, unadd), `tests/test_day_grid.py` (op=add over HTTP, viewer refused), `tests_browser/test_day_new.py` (390 and 320), `tests_browser/test_day_menu.py` (the stale refresh). Screenshots: `F097_SHOTS=<folder> pixi run pytest -p no:randomly tests_browser/test_day_new.py` (create-390.png).

## A small Day | Week switch; the top folds away (F-103)

The captain: the toggle "is very big and occupies a lot of real estate... it's almost like it should fade away, and the only thing I should see is a compact version". Code: `tripcanvas.py` (`toggle`, `head(switch=)`, `fold_bar`), `assets/js/day_fold.js`, styles `.cz-segs`, `.cz-fold` in `trip_canvas.css`.

**The switch.** Day | Week is a 2rem track inside the heading (the dark one on the day, the plain one on the week), between the words and the buttons, with no row of its own; each side is still a 2.75rem target (taller than the track, overlapping only empty heading), the open side a solid pill. A phone's day heading is one row: the chevron is hidden (the Week side is the way up; it stays in the page, so Escape and the keyboard still work), the day count in the kicker and SOS's word drop out below 440px (SOS keeps its icon and accessible name).

**The fold.** The day's heading, kinds row, dates and filters scroll away as they always did. Once the lowest of them has left the top, a compact bar (the day's name, the switch, map, SOS: `.cz-fold-bar`, drawn by the server as a hidden, inert, id-less copy) slides in at the top, below the safe-area inset; scrolling back to the top hides it, a tap on it (its name, or any bare part) scrolls back up and focuses the title. The switch, SOS and map in it work as in the heading. The bar sits in a zero-height sticky box and only its transform and opacity move, so nothing is re-laid-out and the grid's coordinates never change under a finger. `day_fold.js` (a scroll listener through `requestAnimationFrame`) also leaves the bar as it is while a block is held, dragged or resized (`CZ.held`), a hold menu or a title field is open (`CZ.menuOpen`, `CZ.editing()`), and brings it up to date when that is over. A day with plans opens scrolled to them (F-097), so it opens with the bar already in. `html { scroll-padding-top }` keeps what is scrolled into view below the bar. Reduced motion: no slide. The week keeps its full heading.

Tests: `tests/test_day_fold.py` (markup), `tests_browser/test_day_fold.py` (390 and 320, and a laptop: switch size and place, both ways on day and week, fold and unfold by scrolling, the tap, the bar's switch, SOS and map, held and typing keep the bar still, motion and reduced motion).
