# The trip canvas (F-081)

One surface for the trip that zooms: week, day, block (a park day with its parts and steps), step. Module `gitaway/pages/tripcanvas.py`, styles `assets/css/trip_canvas.css`, scripts `assets/js/canvas_core.js` (levels, fetching, swaps, the write transport) with its modules `canvas_filters.js`, `canvas_writes.js`, `canvas_sos.js`, `canvas_gestures.js` and `canvas_tick.js` (F-128). Designs: frames 5, 6, 7 and 11 of `docs/design/canvas/Trip-Canvas-v2.html`, frames 1 to 3 of `Plan-Steps-v1.html`.

## Addresses (every level has one, drawn by the server)

| Level | Address | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Week | `/trip/canvas` | a row per day; park days show their parts as mini blocks with counts and one note sticker; free days have a `+` (editors) |
| Day | `/trip/canvas?day=N` | blocks in time order; a park block shows its parts with steps as chips, notes as stickers on their item, who as avatars; the day's Set aside tray |
| Block | `/trip/canvas?block=aN` | parts as sections, steps in order, lanes where people split up, the block's Set aside tray, the trip's lists |
| Step | `/trip/canvas?step=<id>` | a bottom sheet over its block: note, who is going, Mark done / Not done, Set aside / Put back |

An unknown address goes back to the week. `?frag=1` returns the level alone (no page around it) for the script. `POST /trip/canvas/step` does the four writes (`gitaway.canvas.set_done`, `set_aside`) and, for the script (`X-Canvas: 1`), answers 204 with `X-Canvas-Url`; without script it redirects. Only a `/trip/canvas…` address is accepted as the way back. Writes are gated by `gitaway.access` like every write: a viewer reads every level and sees no buttons and no `+`.

## Where it is reached from

Today (`/trip`) stays the first tab. Its heading has a "Week view" link (`#tp-open-canvas`, every role), and the canvas has a Today | Week | Day control (`Today` leaves the canvas). The phone tab bar is unchanged (the canvas shows Today as the current tab). Replacing Today with the canvas, as the storyboard draws it, is a later decision for the captain once the family has used both.

## The zoom

A tap on a link marked `data-zoom` ("in", "out", "side"), a two-finger pinch, or the browser's Back fetches the level as a fragment and swaps it inside `document.startViewTransition`. The tapped element (`data-zk="day-2" | "blk-a3" | "stp-<id>"`) is named `cz-hero` before the swap and its twin after it, so a day row grows into the day heading, a block card into the block heading, a step chip into the sheet, and back. The page behind scales and fades with a soft blur (`html[data-cz-dir]`), the tab bar stays put. Durations and easing are `--cz-dur` and `--cz-ease` on `:root`.

- No View Transitions: the new level scales and fades in (`.cz-in-in`, `.cz-in-out`, `.cz-in-side`).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: an instant swap, no transition at all.
- Pinch: removed (F-092, it did not work reliably on the captain's iPhone). The Day | Week toggle (`#cz-z-day`, `#cz-z-week`), every row and the back button zoom; Escape zooms out. The canvas sets `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom` (sideways moves are the flick; the page's own pinch zoom works).
- Zooming out to where you came from is the browser's Back (history stays tidy); a write from the sheet zooms out to its block.
- Mark done and Set aside write one coalesced family card ("Abhi finished 3 rides at Universal Studios Hollywood", changed in place for 15 minutes, one push) in `gitaway.canvas._tell`.

## Lanes

Steps of a part that share a time and belong to different people (`who`, with Everyone being nobody in particular) are drawn side by side, one lane per set of people, labelled with the avatars; everything else runs full width (`pages.tripcanvas.slots`).

## Wide day view

At 721px and up the same markup is the laptop view: the week as a strip of day cards across the top (a bar segment per part), the day's parts as lanes (name on the left, rides beside it), the Set aside tray at the side. A strip card swaps the day with a plain cross-fade.

## Touch moves and filters (F-082)

Frame 8 of `Trip-Canvas-v2.html` and frame 4 of `Plan-Steps-v1.html`. Editors only (a viewer has no `data-edit` on `#cz`: no drag, no swipe, no add, no Move; the filters still work).

**Hold and drag** (`canvas_core.js`, pointer events). Hold a step (a day chip, a block row, a Set aside chip) for 350 ms: it dims, a chip follows the finger, and the drop targets light up. Moving more than 10px first is a scroll. Targets: a part (a line shows where it goes; before the step the finger is above, else at the end), a day in the bar that appears at the top (blocks on other days), the Set aside tray along the bottom. Letting go posts `POST /trip/canvas/move` and swaps the level in place (`dir: side`, scroll kept), then the toast ("<title> moved to <where>") with Undo. A step dropped before a timed step takes that step's time (so it can join or leave a lane); at the end of a part it keeps its own. Dropped on another day it lands at the end of that block's same-named part, else its first. Dragging a Set aside chip onto a part puts it back there.

**Undo** posts the snapshot the move returned (`canvas.move_step` -> `undo`) to `POST /trip/canvas/undo`; `canvas.restore` checks every id, part, time and number in it against the open trip and puts those steps back exactly (block, part, place, time, tray). The family card ("Abhi moved 3 rides at Universal", `canvas._tell` column `moved`) is changed in place for 15 minutes and loses the move when it is undone; adds share the same mechanism ("added").

**Swipe.** On a block row, left reveals Done (Not done) and Set aside (the row gives up room instead of sliding off), right or a tap elsewhere puts them away; they are the same forms as the sheet's. **Add a step** is a sheet over the block, `?block=aN&add=1[&part=&title=&note=]` (works without script): a name, who (Everyone is one choice, then each member, names and initials the trip already uses, Adults, Kids), which part, an optional time (the GitAway time picker), a note; `POST /trip/canvas/add`. **Add a note** and **Move** are on the step sheet: the note form (`POST /trip/canvas/note`, the sheet stays) and a Move menu with the same targets as dragging as buttons (earlier or later in the part, each other part, each other day), for a keyboard or a screen reader.

**Filters.** On a day and a block: Everyone, each family member, names and initials the trip uses, Adults and Kids (only when a step is for them: the family's ages are not known), and each named list of the trip. Single choice; the matching steps get a ring, the rest dim. A step for everyone matches every person. A list matches steps by name (`canvas.same_step`: equal, or one starts with the other by whole words, so "Soarin'" is "Soarin' Around the World"). With a list chosen, a card says "4 more from your <list> list aren't in this day yet" with a chip for each: tap to open the add sheet filled in, or drag it onto a part to add it there. The choice is kept per person and trip in `localStorage` (`cz-filter:<trip>:<user>`; it still works for the page if storage is blocked).

**Writes** (`/trip/canvas/step|move|undo|add|note`) are editor-gated by `gitaway.access`, take the trip the page was drawn for (`trip` field), and the script gets JSON `{url, toast, undo}` or 422 `{error}`.

## The day as a time grid; move and resize by touch (F-097)

Brief `docs/briefs/touch-day.md` (the captain: "blocks of time where I can edit with my finger, just like I would edit a Teams meeting"). Code: `tripcanvas.py` (`time_grid`, `grid_block`, `grid_bookings`, `grid_range`, `plan_write`, `POST /trip/canvas/plan`), `gitaway/planedit.py`, `assets/js/day_grid.js`, `assets/css/day_grid.css`.

**The grid.** A day with a plan draws the plans as blocks on an hour grid (7 AM to 10 PM, wider for an earlier or later booking or plan): the block's place and height are its time (`--s` minutes after the grid's first hour, `--l` minutes long, 1 minute = `--mm`; 4.5rem an hour on a phone, 5.5rem on a laptop). Overlaps sit side by side (`calendar.lanes`). Bookings are the quiet dashed lines of F-090/F-093 at their times (`.cz-gbk`, a tap opens the booking's sheet; a line that would sit on another slides below it, its label keeps the true time) and the plans leave a narrow strip free so a booking's icon still shows under them. Today has a now line (`#cz-nowline`, the script moves it). A park block (a plan with parts and steps) is a block too: it moves and resizes as a whole, its parts are small labels (they stay drop targets for a list chip or a set-aside step), a tap opens its block level; its steps are picked up in the block level as before (F-082). A blank day keeps Talk / Paste and its booking lines. The week, the Day | Week toggle, the kind and who/list filters (a block lights when any of its steps match), SOS and the flick between days are unchanged; a laptop gets the same markup wider. A viewer's page has no `data-edit`: no knobs, no menu button. A tap on a plan opens its chat, for everyone (the captain's call); a tap on a park block opens its block level. Every block is at least 2.75rem tall (a 15-minute plan is drawn taller than its time, its words say the true time), a short block fades what does not fit and shows all of itself when focused, held or with its menu. An Undo carries the values the edit left (`undo.after`) and is refused ("Someone changed it since") when another member changed the plan; times and lengths nobody changed are never re-snapped or held to the forms' 30-minute minimum (`tripcal._clean(was=)`), so phone plans stay editable on the desktop and by Ask. Writes time out after 12 s and a failed save puts the block back.

**Gestures** (`day_grid.js`, editors). Hold a block about 350 ms: it lifts (scale and shadow, a light buzz where the browser has `navigator.vibrate`, which iPhone Safari does not), a dashed ghost shows the 15-minute slot it will land in, a label under the finger shows the new time, and the page scrolls when the finger is near the top or the bottom (above the tab bar). Hold the bottom edge instead: the grid zooms in around the finger, about 2.5x, on one layer with a `scaleY` whose origin is the edge being dragged (words and hairlines scale back by `1/--zk`, so they keep their size), 5-minute steps while zoomed, and eases back when the finger lifts; the shortest plan is 15 minutes. Letting go posts `op=edit` with the new `start` and `end` and the block lands in its place (a FLIP), then a quiet refresh makes the page what the server says. Nothing is read from layout inside a frame: the geometry is read once at the lift, then each frame writes a `transform` (move), one block's height (resize) and the zoom, from the latest pointer. Vertical scroll stays native until a hold lifts a block (`touch-action: pan-y`, and the first touchmove after the hold is cancelled); a sideways flick on empty grid, or one that starts on a block before the hold, still changes the day; a held block never flicks (`CZ.held`). Two fingers are never a gesture. Reduced motion: no zoom, no easing, 15-minute steps.

**Saving.** `POST /trip/canvas/plan`, `op` = `edit` (any of `start`, `end` as minutes, `title`), `undo` (the snapshot an edit returned, or `{"deleted": id}`) or `delete`; the script gets JSON `{toast, undo, plan}`, a refusal is 422 `{error}` in the calendar's own words, a viewer is refused by `gitaway.access`. `planedit.change` goes through `tripcal.update_in` with `fine=True`: times snap to 5 minutes and a plan may be 15 minutes long (the forms keep 15 and 30); overlapping is never refused (F-086). The family is told once: the first move writes a card, and the same person's later nudges of that plan inside `canvas.CARD_WINDOW` change that card in place with no second push; an Undo back to where the plan began takes the card away (`planedit._tell`). A plan Ask adds with a start and no length is an hour (`say._hour_long`), and nobody is asked how long.

Tests: `tests/test_planedit.py` (the model), `tests/test_day_grid.py` (the grid and the write over HTTP), `tests_browser/test_day_grid.py` (390 and 320: sizes and lanes, move, resize with the zoom, Undo, edge scroll, a viewer, reduced motion, flick against hold, a real finger, phone checks). ## Hold menu, rename in place, delete (F-098)

Code: `assets/js/day_menu.js` (loads after `day_grid.js`, which hands it `CZ.hold`, `CZ.tap`, `CZ.setTimes`, `CZ.undo`), styles at the end of `day_grid.css`, the write is `POST /trip/canvas/plan` (`op=edit` with `title`, `op=delete`, `op=undo`).

**The hold menu.** Hold a block (or its bottom edge) and let go without moving: it is not moved, a "Fine: 5 min" switch in the menu makes the four moves 5 minutes (precision without the zoom), it wiggles (`rotate`, nothing under reduced motion, where a ring still marks it) and a small card pops under it (over it when there is no room above the tab bar): Chat (a real link to the plan's chat, `data-talk`), Rename, Delete, and Earlier, Later, Shorter, Longer by 15 minutes (the same writes as dragging, so a keyboard and a screen reader have every gesture; a nudge that would leave the grid or go under 15 minutes is disabled). It is `role="menu"` with `role="menuitem"` buttons of at least 44px. A finger that held lands on the menu itself; a keyboard or a screen reader (`.cz-gb-menubtn`, a visually hidden "Change <title>" button on every block, or Enter on a focused plan) lands on the first item, arrows, Home and End move, Escape closes it and gives the focus back to the block (the canvas's own Escape, which zooms out a level, is not reached). A nudge keeps the menu open on the same item: it is put back on the refreshed block. Tapping away puts it away and opens nothing.

**Delete** asks once, in the menu ("Delete Lunch?", and for a park day "Its 16 steps go with it"; Keep it or Delete), then the block fades, and the toast says "Lunch deleted" (or "... and its 16 steps deleted") with Undo (`planedit.undelete`: the plan and its steps are back exactly).

**Rename in place.** Two quick taps on a title, or Rename in the menu: the title becomes a field right there (16px, so a phone does not zoom in; a short block grows while it is edited). Enter or tapping away saves ("Renamed to ..." with Undo), Escape cancels. An empty title is refused with "Give it a title." and one over 40 characters with "Keep the title to 40 characters." (the calendar's own rules): Enter keeps the field open with the reason under it; tapping away from a title that cannot be saved puts the old one back and says why in the toast. A park block's single tap on its title waits a third of a second for a second tap before it opens; every other tap opens at once.

## Make a plan by touching empty time (F-101)

Code: `assets/js/day_new.js` (loads last), the shared title field `CZ.titleField` in `day_menu.js` (Rename uses it too), `planedit.add` / `planedit.unadd`, `POST /trip/canvas/plan` `op=add` (`day`, `start`, `end` in minutes, `title`; Undo is `{"added": id}`), styles `.cz-gb.is-new`, `.cz-gnew` at the end of `day_grid.css`.

Hold empty time of the day grid still for about 350 ms (a scroll, the flick between days, a second finger, a block or a booking line never start it): a one-hour block appears at the 15-minute slot under the finger and follows it; on release its title field opens in place with the keyboard up. Enter or tapping away saves (the calendar's own add rules with the grid's 5-minute grain, overlaps allowed), with a toast and Undo; Escape or an empty title removes it, a title the calendar refuses stays open with the reason (tapping away from it gives it up and says why). A tap on empty time shows a "+" in its slot (gone after 6 s or another tap); pressing it does the same. Keyboard and screen reader: a visually hidden "Add a plan" button first in the grid. Once saved it is a plan like any other (hold to move, hold the bottom edge to resize).

**The iOS rule.** Safari opens the keyboard only for `focus()` inside a user activation (touchend, pointerup, click), not from a timer. The 350 ms timer therefore only shows the block; `openField` runs from the same `pointerup` that ends the hold (or from the click on the "+"), and retries once on the touchend and click that follow if the focus was refused. A blur in the first 350 ms after opening (the mouse events a touch ends with) is taken back, not treated as tapping away; a real tap away (the next `pointerdown`, even on something that is not a control, where iOS would not blur) saves.

While the title is typed the grid is calm (`.is-making`: other blocks and bookings dimmed and untouchable, no gesture, no flick; `CZ.editing()` keeps the revalidation from swapping the page). The family is told once: `planedit.add` tags its "added" card so a move, resize or rename of the new plan inside `canvas.CARD_WINDOW` rewrites that card (still "added", at the new time, no second push); Undo of the add removes the plan and, while it is the newest card, the card.

Stale refreshes: `canvas_core.js` counts writes (`epoch`); a revalidation or a quiet refresh that was fetched before a write and answers after it is dropped (it redrew the block as it was before the previous hold-menu nudge, so Shorter applied twice). A dropped quiet refresh is asked for again, so a failed later write cannot leave the screen stale.

**The copy we already have (F-170, `canvas_core.js`, tests `tests_browser/test_cache_feel.py`).** A level fetched ahead (idle, or as the finger went down) is shown at once whatever its age: there is no freshness limit and a write no longer empties the cache. Each cached copy remembers the write count it was fetched under (`gen`, which moves when a write starts and ends and on `CZ.forget()`) and when. A copy that is older than `STALE` (5 s) or from before a write is *old*: `goto` shows it, and after the zoom `revalidate` asks the server (`fetchLevel(u, true)`, which keeps the old copy usable until the answer lands) and, only if the text differs and the screen is calm, morphs the changed nodes in (`morphIn`: nothing is touched when nothing changed). The API for other scripts:
- `CZ.post(url, body)` (the one write): bumps `gen`; the reply's `level` is both `handed` (the next `CZ.quiet()` needs no request) and written into the cache for its address, so a write never leaves its own level old. After a write the idle prefetch asks again for the old copies of the likeliest levels first (`near`: a day's two neighbours, the week's visible day rows, a block's visible steps), the rest (the week, far days) 300 ms later.
- `CZ.quiet()` / `CZ.refresh()`: the level on the screen, brought up to date in place (uses `handed`, else asks).
- `CZ.forget()`: something changed that no `CZ.post` drew (a chat count, an Ask apply): every copy becomes *old* (still shown at once, then checked). `CZ.forget(true)` also throws every copy away: only for a level that must not flash its old self first (Ask opening another day).

## A clicked plan opens in the panel (F-141)

Design: frame A1 of `docs/design/canvas/Web-Trip-v1.html`. **Outline first, then the words.** The pane opens at once with the title and time read from the block and soft rows, growing out of the block (`GA.motion.open`), and fills in place when the card route answers (`GET /trip/canvas/card?act=aN&panel=1`: the pane and the message bar). A refresh of the day (`CZ.quiet`) leaves the panel alone while it moves, and a message being written, the Add a step form and the Set aside list keep their state (`data-keep-state`, `canvas_core.js` MORPH callback).

**The address keeps the plan.** `/trip/canvas?day=N&plan=<id>` (the day's own address plus `plan=`; `daydock.plan_url`, also `data-base` on the panel for the script). The server draws the day with the panel in plan mode and the block ringed, so a reload (or a link) shows it again; a plan that is not on that day, or not there, is ignored (the panel is idle). The script writes the address in place (`history.replaceState`, no history entry, nothing fetched) when a plan opens or the panel goes back, and every write's `next` is that address, so the refresh after a tick, a step or a note redraws the panel too. Esc or the close button returns the panel to Up next; Esc leaves a note or message being written alone first.

**The mode API** (`window.Dock`, `day_dock.js`; the panel is `#cz-dock[data-mode=idle|plan|family|ask]`):
- `Dock.register(mode, { tab, same(opts, dock), url(opts, dock), enter(opts, from), leave(to) })`: `tab` is the tab it belongs to (idle and plan belong to Plan), `same` says `opts` is already shown (a no-op), `url` the address that keeps it (none given: the day's own), `enter` fills the panel (may return a promise), `leave` puts it away (a promise). A mode adds its own pane inside `#cz-dock` and CSS shows it for its `data-mode` (see how `daydock.panel` draws the idle and plan panes and `day_dock.css` shows one).
- `Dock.show(mode, opts)`: switches mode without a page load: leaves the old mode, sets `data-mode`, moves `aria-selected`/`is-on` to the mode's tab, writes the address, enters the new one, and fires `dock:mode` (`detail: { mode, from, opts }`). Returns `false` when nobody registered the mode.
- `Dock.close()` is `Dock.show('idle')`; `Dock.mode()`, `Dock.busy()`, `Dock.has(mode)`.
- The tabs call it: Plan returns to the plan that was open (or Up next); **Family and Ask call `Dock.show('family' | 'ask')` and, while nothing is registered for them, keep their links (`/trip/family`, `/trip/ask?day=N`). ## Family chat in the panel (F-143)

Design: frame A3 of `docs/design/canvas/Web-Trip-v1.html`, `docs/design/web-trip.md` (Panel: Family). Code: `gitaway/familychats.py` (the chats and what is unread), `gitaway/pages/familydock.py` (the pane and its two routes), `assets/js/day_family.js` (the one `Dock.register('family', ...)`), `assets/css/day_family.css`, a few lines in `daydock.py` (the tab's count, `?panel=family`), `tab_family.py` (the thread route's `scope` and `read`, the hand-over) and `canvas_core.js` (a refresh leaves `[data-live]` alone). From 1020px up (not an installed app) the Family tab and the top bar's Family switch the panel to the trip's chats in place; 721 to 1019 and the phone keep today's Family page exactly.

**The chats.** A chat has a key: `trip` (the whole trip: messages, photos, voice notes and plan-change cards that belong to no plan), `<plan id>`, or `<plan id>:<part id>` (what was said on a plan or a part, F-091). The list shows the whole trip first, then every plan or part with something said, newest first, each with its newest line ("Maya: Eggslut line is short") and an unread count; the open chat has none and shows below the list with its heading (the plan's title and time) and, fixed at the panel's foot, its message bar. **People and invites** (`/family`) sits between the list and the chat. Everyone may write, viewers too. Viewers and editors see the same.

**Nothing is forked.** The chat is `tab_family.fragment` (the Family tab's bubbles) and the phone's own `thread.js`, run again on the pane each time it opens (`#ft`, `#ft-thread`, `#ft-compose`; `ft-close` takes it off): the bubble at once, Retry, the order, and the poll are the phone's. The whole trip's poll and its message POST carry `scope=trip` so only that chat's items come back (`familythread.items(scope="trip")`); a plan's chat uses `/trip/talk/items` and `/trip/talk/message` with its `act` and `part`. Without `scope` the Family tab's routes answer exactly as before.

**Unread.** Marks are per person, per trip and per chat (`thread_reads`, `docs/family-db.md`; plan ids repeat and row numbers run on across a family's trips, so reading one trip never touches another's): a message is unread when someone else wrote it and it is newer than that chat's mark and than the mark `*`. A chat is read when its pane is drawn for a laptop (`/trip/family/panel`) or started (`day_family.js` posts `/trip/family/seen` with `upto`; the day drawn from `?panel=family` marks nothing itself, and below 1020px the script does not start or keep the pane at all), and by every poll that asks with `read=1`: the panel's chats, a plan's pane, and the phone's own Family tab and plan chat page (`data-poll-url` carries it; a hidden page does not poll). The phone's Family page also marks `*` once when it opens (`POST /trip/family/seen`, on OPEN_POSTS). The Family tab shows the total, leaving out the open chat.

**The address.** `/trip/canvas?day=N&panel=family[&thread=<key>]` (`tc.curl(day=N, panel="family", thread=key)`): the server draws the day with the chats open (the top bar marks Family), the script keeps the address in place as chats are picked, a reload shows it again, and picking another day keeps the chats open. A plan in the address wins. **`/trip/family`** (and `/trip/family/thread` opened in the browser's address bar: a navigation is redirected there; the page's own polling is still the bare fragment) hands a laptop on to `?panel=family` for today's day (a short script, the first thing in the page's body: FastHTML keeps scripts out of the head, and nothing is drawn or fetched before it), and a phone or tablet stays on the page it always was. The top bar's Family keeps its address (`/trip/family`): on the day it switches the panel in place, anywhere else it goes there and on to the day.

**Live.** While the Family or the Plan pane is open and the tab is visible, every 5 s (the phone's `POLL_MS`): the open Family chat is polled by `thread.js`; the rows and the tab's count are asked again (`GET /trip/family/list?open=KEY&day=N`, `X-Family-Unread` has the total); and the plan pane's chat (F-141) asks `/trip/talk/items?act=&since=&read=1` and appends what others said (once, by `data-n`) and refreshes the block's chat count. A hidden tab asks for nothing and asks once when it is shown again; Up next asks for nothing. A message that arrives is simply there: no motion (F-118).

## A mouse and a keyboard on the desktop day (F-144)
From 1020px a mouse needs no hold (`CZ.mouseOn(e)` in `day_grid.js`: a `mouse` pointer and the desktop query; a touch screen of any size keeps the phone's gestures).
- **Draw** (`day_new.js`): press empty time and drag past 6px; a dashed outline (`.cz-gb.is-new.is-drawing`) with its times stretches from where the button went down, up or down, both ends on the 15-minute grid, never under 15 minutes. Letting go opens the title field in place (the same `CZ.titleField` as the phone), Enter saves (`op=add`, with Undo), Escape drops it, in the air or in the field. A plain click still shows the "+".
- **Move** (`day_grid.js`): press a plan and drag past 6px; a click or a small wobble opens it in the panel as before. 15-minute snap, the time label by the pointer, the same save (`op=edit`, `X-Canvas-Level`) and Undo toast. **Resize**: the knob at the plan's foot (`.cz-g-tknob`, built on hover by `day_mouse.js`) is dragged at once; no zoom (a mouse is exact), same snap and save. Escape puts a plan held in the air back.
- **Hover and menu** (`day_mouse.js`, `day_mouse.css`): hovering a plan builds `.cz-g-tools` next to it (a layer the size of the block, as the phone's knobs layer): the knob and a menu button. The button and a right-click on a plan call `CZ.hold(block)`: the phone's hold menu (Rename, Delete, nudges), not a second menu. Viewers (`data-edit` absent) get neither.
- **The card scrolls, not the window**: `CZ.dayBox()` is the grid card (`.cz-view[data-day] .cz-day-body`); a lifted plan, a new outline and a lifted step (`canvas_gestures.js`) scroll it when held within 3rem of its top or foot edge, and read `scrollTop` for their geometry. A wheel turn during a hold repaints, so the plan stays under the pointer. `day_card.js` fetches the cards ahead of what is in the card's view and listens to the card's scroll.
- **Keys** (`day_keys.js`, the one place; `CZ.keys` maps a key to a function): T today, left and right arrows the day before and after, W week, D day, `/` Ask. Nothing fires while typing, with a modifier, or with a menu, sheet, card, hold or draft open. `/` calls `Dock.show('ask', {})` when the Ask mode is registered (F-142 only has to `Dock.register('ask', ...)`), else follows the Ask tab's link. Esc is not in this file: each inner thing closes on its own Escape first (a plan in the air or a draft being drawn, the plan menu, a note being written), then `day_dock.js` closes the panel.
- Tests: `tests_browser/test_day_mouse.py` (a real mouse at 1440 and 1280). Screenshots on request: `F144_SHOTS=<folder>`.

## Drag a plan onto a day chip (F-151)
On a laptop a plan held with the mouse (F-144) can be dropped on a day chip in the ink header (`day_chipdrop.js`, called from `day_grid.js`; the chips carry `data-move-day` and `data-move-label`, `tripcanvas.head_chips`). While it is held the row shows dashed outlines, the chip under the pointer gets a coral ring and the label says "Move to Sat 17", the open day's chip does not accept. Held near either end of a row that scrolls (a long trip) the row scrolls. Over the header with no chip under the pointer the plan keeps its time. Bookings (`.cz-gbk`) and steps are not part of it; the phone is untouched.
- **The write** is the existing `POST /trip/canvas/plan` with `op=edit` and a `day` (no start or end: same times); toast "Moved to Sat 17" with Undo (`planedit.restore` already puts the day back); a day outside the trip is a 422. Overlaps on the target day are lanes as always.
- **In place**: the block shrinks into the chip while the write runs, then the day is refreshed through `CZ.quiet()`; no page load. Esc mid-drag puts the plan back (F-144's ordering).
- **The panel**: a plan open in the panel when it is moved sends the panel back to Up next (it would otherwise show a plan that is not on this day); moving a different plan leaves the panel alone. We do not follow the plan to its new day: the captain asked to move it away, not to go there.
- Tests: `tests/test_day_chip_drop.py`, `tests_browser/test_day_chip_drop.py` (a real mouse at 1440 and 1280). Screenshots on request: `F151_SHOTS=<folder>`.

## Change type and Move to day in the plan menu (F-152)
The one plan menu (`day_menu.js`: the phone's hold menu, the laptop's ⋯ and right-click) has a second row, **Change type** and **Move to day…** (plans only, `a…` ids; viewers have no menu at all). Each opens a second step inside the same menu (`toStep`, risen in with `GA.motion.settle`; **Back** returns to the first step, Escape closes the whole menu): no dropdown.
- **Change type**: the five kinds (`tripcal.KINDS`) as large choices with their icon and colour, the plan's own marked (`aria-checked`, a ring). The choices are rendered once per day by the server (`tripcanvas.kind_choices`, hidden `#cz-kinds-tpl`, editors only) so the icons are the app's; the block says its kind in `data-type`.
- **Move to day…**: the trip's days as chips (dow and date; the labels come from the day pills, which carry `data-move-day` and `data-move-label` like the laptop's header chips). The plan's own day is shown marked and is not a button.
- **The write** is the canvas's own `POST /trip/canvas/plan` `op=edit` with `kind` or `day` (`planedit.change(kind=)`; `tripcal` refuses an unknown kind, 422). Toasts: "Lunch is now Food" and F-151's "Moved to Sat 17", both with Undo (`planedit.restore` puts the kind back; the snapshot carries `kind`, optional for older snapshots). The grid and the colour refresh in place (`CZ.quiet()`). A move closes the laptop panel back to Up next when that plan was open (F-151's rule); a type change keeps it open and updates it.
- Tests: `tests/test_plan_menu.py`, `tests_browser/test_plan_menu.py` (390 with a finger, 1440 with a mouse). Screenshots on request: `F152_SHOTS=<folder>`.
