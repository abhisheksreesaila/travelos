===== Economics 
VERDICT: At 150 creators the affiliate funnel brings GitAway about $25 to $70 a month and gives creators almost nothing, which is roughly the Railway bill. The reasons are in the code: flights go to Aviasales, which gets about 10% of its traffic from the US and pays 1.1% of the fare, from an origin hard-coded to SFO; Klook is wired only to generic "Los Angeles hotels" and car searches; Disneyland tickets are not linked at all; and forks carry no Book links. The Haiku cost claim holds ($0.0014 to $0.007 a draft), and the creator-marker "leak" is small and worth giving away, so this is a content-acquisition bet, not a revenue line, until two cheap fixes land: a Klook things-to-do (Disneyland tickets) link, and the creator handle in the SubID.

===== Trust and legal

The real risks are trust gaps that are cheap to close before F-182 ships: ownership is a checkbox that hands over a permanent handle; the outreach message promises the marker on all Book buttons when only flights carry it; there is no DMCA agent and reports alert no one; the grounding check misses plausible invented names; and the founder's trip publishes plan titles word for word with no check for his kids' names.
