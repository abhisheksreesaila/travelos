# Plan it for me: facts checked against official sites (F-197)

Checked on 2026-11-06 by reading the official pages below (a few through search results on the official domain, marked "via search"). The example plans (`gitaway/planexamples.py`), the "What to book and when" checklists and the facts handed to the agent (`gitaway/planfacts.py`) use only what is VERIFIED here. No prices and no opening hours are used anywhere. Re-check before a busy season: parks rename and close rides.

## Disneyland Resort (all on https://disneyland.disney.go.com)

- Parks: Disneyland Park, Disney California Adventure Park, Downtown Disney District. `/`, `/hotels-overview/`
- Hotels: Disneyland Hotel, Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa, Pixar Place Hotel. `/hotels-overview/`
- Height minimums (attraction pages `/attractions/disneyland/<slug>/`, `/attractions/disney-california-adventure/<slug>/`): Space Mountain 40 in; Matterhorn Bobsleds 42; Big Thunder Mountain Railroad 40; Indiana Jones Adventure 46; Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance 40; Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run 38; Tiana's Bayou Adventure 40; Chip 'n' Dale's GADGETcoaster 35; Autopia 32 to ride, 54 to drive alone; Incredicoaster 48; Guardians of the Galaxy – Mission: BREAKOUT! 40; Radiator Springs Racers 40; Soarin' Across America 40 (the current Soarin' film; Soarin' Around the World is not running); Goofy's Sky School 42; Grizzly River Run 42; Jumpin' Jellyfish 40; Mater's Junkyard Jamboree 32; Luigi's Rollickin' Roadsters 32; Pixar Pal-A-Round no minimum.
- No height minimum and running: Dumbo the Flying Elephant, King Arthur Carrousel, Peter Pan's Flight, Mad Tea Party, Casey Jr. Circus Train, Storybook Land Canal Boats, Alice in Wonderland, The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh, Buzz Lightyear Astro Blasters, Finding Nemo Submarine Voyage, Jungle Cruise, Pirates of the Caribbean, Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway; Toy Story Midway Mania!, Monsters, Inc. Mike & Sulley to the Rescue!, Jessie's Critter Carousel, Inside Out Emotional Whirlwind, WEB SLINGERS: A Spider-Man Adventure, The Little Mermaid ~ Ariel's Undersea Adventure.
- Not used in the examples, because they change with the season or are closed: "it's a small world" (Holiday version from Nov 13, 2026), Haunted Mansion (Holiday version; the regular one returns early 2027), Redwood Creek Challenge Trail (closed for refurbishment), Red Car Trolley (status unknown).
- Lightning Lane (`/lightning-lane-passes/`): Lightning Lane Multi Pass, bought in the Disneyland app on the day or in advance with tickets; Lightning Lane Single Pass, in the app on the day only (Rise of the Resistance, Radiator Springs Racers); Lightning Lane Premier Pass, up to 7 days ahead in very limited numbers. All need a ticket and a park reservation.
- Dining reservations: up to 60 days ahead, on the website or in the Disneyland app. `/dining/disneyland/plaza-inn/`, `/guest-services/priority-reservations-hotel-guests/`
- Park reservations: every guest 3 and up needs a park reservation plus a ticket (or Magic Key) for the same park and date. `/guest-services/park-reservations/`
- Rider Switch: one adult rides while the other waits with a child who is too small; then the second adult rides without waiting in line again. `/guest-services/rider-switch/`
- Mobile Order in the Disneyland app at participating restaurants. `/guest-services/mobile-food-orders/`
- Baby Care Centers: Disneyland Park at the end of Main Street, U.S.A., next to First Aid; California Adventure in San Fransokyo Square, next to Ghirardelli. `/guest-services/baby-center-lost-children/`
- Early entry: not offered on the current pages. Disneyland Resort hotel guests get the "Lightning Lane Entry Benefit" (one Multi Pass entry). `/guest-services/early-admission-options/`, `/guest-services/hotel-park-benefits/`
- Night shows on select nights: Fantasmic! (`/entertainment/disneyland/fantasmic/`), World of Color – ONE (`/entertainment/disney-california-adventure/world-of-color-one/`), fireworks whose name changes with the season (`/entertainment/disneyland/fireworks/`).

## New York City

- Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island: Statue City Cruises is the only authorized seller (https://www.nps.gov/stli/planyourvisit/index.htm); boats leave from Castle Clinton in Battery Park and from Liberty State Park, NJ (https://www.statuecitycruises.com/); pedestal and crown need reserved tickets bought ahead, crown tickets are not sold on the island; "Crown sell out 90-days in advance" (Statue City Cruises); the crown is 162 steps, no elevator, children at least 42 in (https://www.nps.gov/stli/planyourvisit/visit-the-crown.htm); airport-style screening before boarding (https://www.nps.gov/stli/planyourvisit/safety.htm).
- American Museum of Natural History (via search): advance tickets recommended (https://www.amnh.org/plan-your-visit); dinosaur halls on the 4th floor (https://www.amnh.org/dinosaurs/the-dinosaurs-on-display); the blue whale in the Milstein Hall of Ocean Life (https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/permanent/ocean-life); the Gilder Center with its insectarium and butterfly vivarium (https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/permanent/gilder-center).
- Central Park (https://www.centralparknyc.org/locations/...): Belvedere Castle; Bethesda Terrace (its fountain is off for restoration from Aug 2026, about a year); Heckscher Playground (open; its ballfields closed until spring 2027); the Central Park Carousel (weather permitting); Central Park Zoo, run by WCS (https://centralparkzoo.com/).
- The High Line, Gansevoort St to 34th St, elevators at Gansevoort, 14th, 23rd and 30th Sts; no bikes, skateboards or scooters (https://www.thehighline.org/faq/, https://www.thehighline.org/visit/).
- 9/11 Memorial & Museum: museum tickets are timed and bought ahead (https://www.911memorial.org/visit/visit-museum-1); the historical exhibition "may not be appropriate for visitors younger than 10" (https://911memorial.org/visit/groups).
- Top of the Rock (via search): timed tickets for a set date and entry window (https://www.rockefellercenter.com/tickets/top-of-the-rock-observation-deck/).
- Brooklyn Bridge promenade stays open during the rehab work (https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/infrastructure/brooklyn-bridge.shtml); Brooklyn Bridge Park and Jane's Carousel in DUMBO, in a glass pavilion, year-round (https://www.brooklynbridgepark.org/attractions/janes-carousel/).
- Intrepid Museum: the space shuttle Enterprise in the Space Shuttle Pavilion (https://www.intrepidmuseum.org/).
- Subway: OMNY tap to pay with a card, phone or watch; MetroCards are no longer sold (since Jan 1, 2026) (https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/subway-bus, .../metrocard). The children's rule is a fare rule, so it is left out.
- Time zone America/New_York.

## Oahu

- Hanauma Bay Nature Preserve (https://www.honolulu.gov/dpr/hanauma-bay-nature-preserve/): visitors from out of state book admission 2 days ahead on the city's booking site (a share is kept for walk-ins); the educational video is required on every visit; it is closed some days each week ("check which days it is open"). Safety page: many incidents happen in shallow water, sharp coral near shore, children must always be supervised.
- Diamond Head State Monument: out-of-state visitors need a reservation, up to 30 days ahead (https://gostateparks.hawaii.gov/diamondhead/faq); 0.8 mi, steep, the last part all stairs, through a tunnel; bring water, a hat and sunscreen (https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/parks/oahu/diamond-head-state-monument/).
- Pearl Harbor National Memorial: USS Arizona Memorial tickets release 8 weeks ahead, with a second release the day before, only on recreation.gov; everyone over age 1 needs one (https://www.nps.gov/perl/uss-arizona-memorial-programs.htm); no bags; strollers go only as far as the pre-boarding area, so a carrier is suggested (https://www.nps.gov/perl/faq-s.htm).
- Places: Waikiki Aquarium (https://www.waikikiaquarium.org/), Honolulu Zoo in Kapiolani Park (https://www.honoluluzoo.org/), Magic Island lagoon in Ala Moana Regional Park, calm water for kids (https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/oahu/things-to-do/beaches/magic-island-lagoon), Kailua Beach Park, Ko Olina lagoons (public may use them, limited public parking, no lifeguards: https://www.koolina.com/destination/lagoons/), Dole Plantation, Haleiwa (https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/oahu/regions/north-shore/haleiwa), Waimea Valley, Kualoa Ranch, Polynesian Cultural Center (closed some days each week).
- Sea turtles: stay at least 10 ft (3 m) away, on land and in the water (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/pacific-islands/marine-life-viewing-guidelines/viewing-marine-wildlife-hawaii).
- Sunscreen: selling sunscreen with oxybenzone or octinoxate is illegal in Hawaii without a prescription (HRS §342D-21, https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol06_Ch0321-0344/HRS0342D/HRS_0342D-0021.htm).
- Car seats (HRS §291-11.5, https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol05_Ch0261-0319/HRS0291/HRS_0291-0011_0005.htm): under 2 rear-facing; 2 to under 4 a harnessed seat; 4 to under 10 a harnessed seat or a booster.
- Time zone Pacific/Honolulu: HST, no daylight saving time (https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/local-time-faqs).
