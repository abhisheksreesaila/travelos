# F-187: family-travel creators to invite

The list is `creators.csv` in this folder: 50 creators, 12 priority A, 18 B and 20 C. Nobody has been contacted.

## How they were chosen

- **Places.** Each creator has at least one public post or video about one of our six launch places: Disneyland / Disney California Adventure, Los Angeles with kids, San Diego, Oahu/Maui, Lake Tahoe, or New York with kids. Many cover two or more.
- **Itinerary-shaped content.** I preferred day-by-day plans ("3 days in...", "a week in Oahu with kids") over lists, because a day plan is what becomes a forkable trip. `example_post_url` is the post that would convert best.
- **Size.** I favoured small and mid creators (roughly 5k to 200k audience) and added a few large ones as long shots: Busy Toddler, Mommy Poppins, Disney Tourist Blog, Stroller in the City.
- **Found by web search, checked on their own sites.** `contact_url` is their own contact, work-with-me or media-kit page. A creator's YouTube About page is used where they have no website contact. No personal emails were collected.
- **Every link checked.** All 150 URLs (main, example, contact) returned HTTP 200 on 2026-11-05. lajollamom.com blocks curl but loads in a normal fetch. These were dropped because their pages would not load or the fit was wrong:
  - Mint Arrow and The DIY Playbook: pages blocked or would not load.
  - Endless Travel Plans: a family trip-planning app, so a competitor rather than a creator.
  - The Family Vacation Guide: no named creator; looks like a content farm.
  - Eat Live Run: no posts since 2020.
  - Learning Escapes: Europe-based.
  - Tips from the Disney Diva: the domain now redirects to an unrelated site.
  - The Cards We Drew: mostly recipes, and says it doesn't answer outreach.
- **Priority.** A means a strong place fit, itinerary posts, active in 2025-26, small or mid sized, with a clear way to reach them. B means a good fit with one weakness. C covers large, dormant, local-media, partial-fit or hard-to-reach creators.

## Contact these 10 first

1. **Hawaii Travel with Kids / Marcie in Mommyland (Marcie Cheung).** Covers three of our places (Hawaii, Disneyland, NYC) with day-by-day itineraries. She publishes 80k monthly pageviews and a 10k email list, and already earns from affiliate links.
2. **Trips With Tykes (Leslie Harvey).** A Bay Area mom who writes about Tahoe and Disneyland. Her media kit is current (summer 2026, 60k pageviews) and LA is her top reader city. She is small enough to reply.
3. **La Jolla Mom (Katie Dillon).** The leading San Diego family-trip voice, with a planning guide that maps straight onto a trip. She also covers Disneyland tickets. Her YouTube has 13.4K subscribers.
4. **The Wandering Daughter (Astrid Vinje).** Writes several itinerary variants per place (San Diego, Disneyland), and each one could be its own forkable trip.
5. **Your Brooklyn Guide (Megan Indoe).** A real 3-day NYC itinerary with kids, from a local guide with about 26K on Instagram.
6. **SoCal Disney Dad.** A Disneyland YouTube channel (91K subscribers) aimed at parents of young kids. He already earns from travel affiliate links, so his own marker on bookings is a pitch he will understand. He has no public email, so reach him through the channel.
7. **The Hawaii Vacation Guide.** Already sells Hawaii itineraries; their Maui-with-a-toddler week is a ready trip. About 31K on Instagram and 8.5K on YouTube.
8. **Life With My Littles.** About 242K monthly pageviews, a Disneyland-with-toddlers guide and a clear partnerships page.
9. **Kids Are A Trip (Kirsten Maxwell).** One of the few family creators with full Tahoe and NYC plans. Publishes 50K pageviews a month and 150K+ followers.
10. **No Back Home.** An LA family blog covering LA with kids and Disneyland/DCA with toddlers. Active in 2026 and publishes a partnership address.

Next in line: Travels Between (Colleen Lanin, formerly Travel Mamas) and Mommy Mouse Clubhouse.

## Caveats

- **Audience numbers are estimates.** Most are the creator's own figures from a media kit or advertise page, often undated; some are years old (Rockin Mama 2013, Have Baby Will Travel 2019). Others are public Instagram or YouTube counts read on 2026-11-05. `unknown` means nothing was published. Verify before ranking by size.
- **Coverage gaps.** Lake Tahoe and Oahu/Maui have few active small family creators. Most Tahoe entries are B or C, and only Marcie Cheung and The Hawaii Vacation Guide are strong for Hawaii. TikTok and Instagram-only creators are under-represented: their follower counts and contacts can't be checked without logging in, so the list leans toward blogs and YouTube.
- **Ownership and activity change.** Kids Are A Trip changed owner (from Jody Halsted to Kirsten Maxwell). Travel Mamas became Travels Between. It's a Lovely Life, Thrifty Family Travels, Rockin Mama and BonBon Break look dormant.
- **Some may see us as competitors.** Ciao Bambino, 2 Travel Dads and Families Love Travel sell trip planning or agency services.
- **Who earns from bookings.** Several creators are outside the US (Bridges and Balloons in the UK, Thrifty Family Travels in Australia), which may change where their readers book. Busy Toddler and Mommy Poppins are long shots.
