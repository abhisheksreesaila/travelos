# No platform usernames — anonymous publishing with external social attribution

TravelOS community plans are published without a platform identity. The contributor is identified only by the plan itself—not by a TravelOS username or profile. Professional creators may optionally attach their existing social channels (YouTube, Instagram) as attribution, but those are external identities.

**Why:** Platform usernames create account management, profile pages, reputation systems, moderation targets, and identity verification—all before the core loop (plan → collaborate → share → publish) is proven. By making every plan anonymous by default, the plan must stand on its own quality. External social handles give professional creators attribution without coupling their identity to our platform. This is hard to reverse because introducing usernames later means migrating from an anonymous model to an identified one, which changes discovery ranking, trust signals, and the creator incentive structure.

## Considered Options

- **Platform usernames with profiles (rejected):** The GitHub model—every published plan has an owner with a username, avatar, and profile page. Rejected because it front-loads identity infrastructure and makes the platform about people rather than plans. A plan with a famous creator's name on it gets forked regardless of quality; an anonymous plan must earn every fork.

- **Hybrid: optional usernames (rejected):** Let creators opt into profiles. Rejected because optional identity creates a two-tier system where "verified" creators dominate discovery and anonymous contributors are invisible. Worse than either extreme.

- **Anonymous-only with external attribution (chosen):** All plans are anonymous. The plan itself carries quality signals (forks, views). Professional creators attach YouTube/Instagram handles that link out to platforms they already maintain. No new identity to manage.
