## 3. What a creator sees in the first minute

Land (https://gitaway.me/guides/make, linked from "Make a guide" on /guides)
- The lead: "Your post becomes a guide people can plan and book from. Your words and your links stay yours."
- One paste box, four short reasons (another place your post travels, readers can plan the trip, the links stay yours, you can see who reads it), the terms in plain words, the questions bloggers ask.

Paste
- With the AI key set (not on gitaway.me yet): paste a blog link, YouTube link or Instagram caption; the draft lands in place (title and picture, the post's own sentences, days and stops), then "Open your guide".
- Without it (gitaway.me today): the page says "Reading a link is not switched on yet. Paste the text of your post and lay it out yourself." The box takes the text, and "Write it myself" opens the editor. A creator who would rather not do that sends their post to the founder, who drafts it privately in /admin/draft and sends back a one-use link (/claim/...) to check, fix and publish. That is a manual step by the founder, so say so when you offer it.

Guide
- The creator's own page at /trips/<slug>: the post is the first tile ("From the blog" with the site and a link back), their name is the byline, each stop shows their own sentence and a "<Name>'s link" where they gave one. Nothing goes public until they tick "This is my post" and press Publish; they can unpublish in one tap.

Share
- After publishing: the guide's link, a 1080 x 1350 card for Instagram, and a badge with its embed code for the blog.

After
- /creators/stats (sign-in needed) shows readers, plans started and clicks per guide, with no cookies.

## 4. Honest limits to state, not hide

- Reading a link needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, which production does not have yet. Until it does, a creator pastes text or the founder drafts the guide by hand.
- There is no founder guide until the founder publishes one; do not send before it exists, since the email points to it.
- Flights from a guide carry the creator's own Travelpayouts marker only if they add it in their creator settings; stays and things to do support GitAway for now (the page says so).
- No payment to or from creators exists. Paid placement is a possible long way off and is not mentioned to creators.
