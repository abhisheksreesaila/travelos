# Cover storyboard, draft 1 (2026-11-07)

**Style:** Meng To's sketchbook (threeui.com/landing-pages/meng-to-sketchbook-landing-page): ink and watercolor on warm paper.
- The story is told inside a sketchbook. Each scroll turns a page.
- Color comes in where life happens: the widgets, the trip, the world.

**The order the captain set:** book it informed → go → leave a trail (anonymously) → someone far away follows it → the world keeps sharing.

**After the story (the scroll keeps going): the summary.**
1. **Book with everything in view:** weather, money, currency, visas, maps and trails, beside every booking.
2. **One screen for the whole trip:** the plan, every booking, directions, and family chat.
3. **Follow a trail:** start from a trip a real family took, and make it yours.
4. **Leave a trail:** share it anonymously; the next family says thanks.
5. **Free.** Booking happens on the partner's site.
6. Then sign in, or Start a trip.

**Open questions for the captain:**
- Does the second couple follow the same Tahoe trail in summer (beach at Sand Harbor), or a different, beach trip?
- Should currency and visa show for Tahoe? They only make sense abroad. Option: Maya and Sam's trip is abroad (Lisbon?), and Rosa and Tomás live near Tahoe.
- Story length: 13 scenes over about 4 to 5 scrolls (2 to 3 scenes per scroll), or trim to 8?
- Names: Maya and Sam, Rosa and Tomás. Keep or change?
