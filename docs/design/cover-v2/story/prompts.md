# Cover v2 story: image prompts

Every image is generated by `tools/gen.py <job>` (Gemini `generateContent`, `responseModalities: ["IMAGE"]`,
`imageConfig.aspectRatio` 16:9). Model: `gemini-3-pro-image`, falling back to `gemini-3.1-flash-image`.
The prompts below are written from `tools/jobs.py`; edit that file, then run `pixi run python tools/write_prompts.py`.

Regenerate one image:

```sh
cd docs/design/cover-v2/story
GEMINI_KEY_FILE=/path/to/keyfile pixi run python tools/gen.py 04
```

Regenerate everything (sheets first, the panels use them as references): `GEMINI_KEY_FILE=... sh tools/run_all.sh`.
The previous take of an image moves to `takes/`.

Reference images, in order: `refs/style.jpg` (Meng To's sketchbook, cropped from the founder's screenshot),
then, for panels, `characters/maya-sam.png` and `characters/rosa-tomas.png`.

## sheet-maya-sam -> `characters/maya-sam.png`

References: `refs/style.jpg`. Aspect 16:9.

```text
Reference image: the style to match. Draw a CHARACTER SHEET for an animated story, on an open sketchbook spread, in the style of the reference. Two characters, a couple: MAYA: South Asian woman, early 30s, warm brown skin, long dark wavy hair usually in a loose low bun with a few strands falling out, dark expressive eyes, small gold stud earrings, slim. Home: mustard-yellow knit sweater, jeans. Travel (Lake Tahoe in winter): rust-red puffer jacket, cream knit beanie, dark jeans, snow boots. SAM: Black man, early 30s, deep brown skin, short cropped hair with a neat short beard, kind eyes, broad smile, tall and lean. Home: heather-gray hoodie. Travel (Tahoe winter): forest-green parka, charcoal knit beanie, plaid scarf. Layout: left page: each character standing full figure in home clothes, three views each (front, three-quarter, side), consistent proportions. Right page: top row, head-and-shoulders expressions: both puzzled, both delighted, and the two of them looking at each other smiling; bottom row: both full figure in their Tahoe winter travel outfits. Same faces in every drawing. Natural, attractive, believable adults with realistic proportions, not caricatured. Ink linework with soft watercolor; their clothes carry the color (mustard, rust red, forest green), skin tones painted warmly. No labels or text anywhere.

STYLE (match the first reference image exactly): a hand-drawn travel sketchbook page. Fine black ink-pen linework, confident and slightly loose, with light hatching; loose transparent watercolor washes that bleed past the lines and leave white paper showing; warm cream textured watercolor paper. Restrained palette of warm ochre, soft sand and slate blue unless told otherwise. Elegant, editorial, adult illustration, like an urban sketcher's journal. Not cartoonish, not anime, not cute chibi, no thick outlines, no digital gradients, no glossy 3D, no photorealism.
```
