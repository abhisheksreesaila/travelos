# What a reader pulls out of a San Diego itinerary post

Read 2026-11-15 for the guides v2 prototype. These notes cover each post's shape, not its words; the prototype's sample guide is made up.

Sources: [2TravelDads, three days](https://2traveldads.com/three-days-in-san-diego-itinerary/), [Destinationless Travel, three days](https://destinationlesstravel.com/three-days-in-san-diego-itinerary/), [This Life of Travel, 3 days](https://www.thislifeoftravel.com/usa-west-coast/3-day-itinerary-san-diego). Two more (Bridges and Balloons, Never Ending Footsteps) refused automated reads.

1. **Days are themed by area.** "Coastline", "Balboa Park", "History", or split into morning, afternoon and evening. Readers want the day's theme and its order before any detail.
2. **The same neighbourhoods keep coming up.** Gaslamp, Little Italy, Balboa Park, Old Town, La Jolla, Point Loma / Sunset Cliffs, Coronado, North and South Park, Mission Beach. Each stop needs its neighbourhood as a tag.
3. **Each stop's "what" and "why" are buried in a paragraph.** A reader skims for the one line of why (the signature dish, the sea lions at the cove).
4. **Time is the scarcest fact.** Usually missing; where it appears, it's a start time ("food tour 5:30 pm", "cruise 5 to 8") or a length ("0.6 mile walk"). A guide should always show it.
5. **Cost is almost never given per stop**, only "reasonably priced" or a hotel range. A $ band per stop and a budget band per guide would beat every post we read.
6. **The tips are the gold**, and they are scattered in bold: arrive at the zoo at opening, Cabrillo closes in the late afternoon, park at the trailhead, swap days if a tour moves. Make them a tip badge on each stop.
7. **Booking links are the creator's income.** Viator for tours, Booking.com for hotels in three tiers, Discover Cars, an eSIM referral, and otherwise official sites and Google Maps. Show them as the creator's own links, labelled as theirs.
8. **Where to stay comes in three tiers** (luxury, mid-range, budget), with a neighbourhood and a "from $/night" price each, or not at all.
9. **Getting around is one scattered line:** a car for some days, the trolley, rideshare for short hops, scooters where parking is scarce. Worth one compact row per guide.
10. **Season, budget and who it suits are only implied** (whales in winter, swimming mid-May to October, "families", "foodies"). Put them in the at-a-glance header, where readers look first.
11. **The posts are long.** A table of contents, a photo per stop, disclosures, related reads and share buttons. The day's skeleton should fit in one screen, with the detail one tap away (master-detail).
