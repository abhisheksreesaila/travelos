# One product: creators and booking

Design artifacts only, no app code. This applies the approved **Ticket line** cockpit ([`../cockpit/3-ticket.html`](../cockpit/3-ticket.html)) and the cover ([`../cover3d/`](../cover3d/)) to every creator and booking screen, in Direction A ([`../reskin/README.md`](../reskin/README.md)) on omxqua. Brief: [`docs/briefs/free-and-polished.md`](../../briefs/free-and-polished.md) (sharing is the soul; "Take a trip. Leave a trail."; booking is links only).

**Open from disk, no server:** [`index.html`](index.html) shows every screen at desktop (1440) and phone (390) side by side, with a Light/Dark switch. One artboard at real size: `screen.html?s=<screen>&size=dk|ph&mode=light|dark` (screens `creators pick drafting editor trail profile stats claim admin where book paste detail`; for `trail` add `&theme=structured|scrapbook|magazine|minimal`). PNGs are in [`png/`](png), named `<screen>[-theme]-<1440|390>-<light|dark>[-full].png`; `-full` is the whole scrolling page. Rebuild them with `node tools/shoot.mjs [screen…]` (it uses the cockpit's Playwright setup), then `magick mogrify -colors 256 png/*.png`.

**Sample data:** Juno Park, `@junoparkoutside` and `junoparkoutside.com` are a **made-up creator**, labelled "Sample creator" on every screen she appears on. Her counts (214 thanks, 38 follows) are sample numbers. The booking screens use the founder's real Tahoe plan: Dec 19–26 2026, SFO→RNO, 2 adults and kids 8 and 11. The photos are the cover's own 3D renders (`../cover3d/stills/`), standing in for a creator's photos. Night renders are used in dark mode.

**Built on the cockpit, not beside it:** every artboard loads `../cockpit/core/base.css` (tokens, top islands, tab bar, buttons, badges, the ticket stub, the stamp) and `../cockpit/core/icons.js`. [`op.css`](op.css) adds only what these screens need, and [`op.js`](op.js) draws them. Screens 11 and 12 aren't redrawn: `screen.html` hands them to the cockpit's approved Book and Paste screens.

## The system (one set of rules for all 13 screens)
| Rule | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **One canvas** | Neutral grey `#F2F2F5` in light, black in dark. Opaque white or `#1C1C1F` cards on it, with one shadow scale (`--sh1/2/3`). No glass, blur or translucency anywhere, the phone tab bar included. |
| **Colour means a kind** | Flight indigo, stay purple, car green, tickets orange, see teal, food pink, weather blue, ride yellow. These are the cockpit's tokens. They fill kind tiles, time-rail blocks, passes and lane icons. A day never gets a colour: day chips stay neutral, and the selected one is ink. |
| **Ink is the accent** | One filled ink button per card. The focal card on each screen is an **ink ticket** (inverse in dark mode): the picks on 2, the drafting progress on 3, the publish checklist on 4, the thanks on 7, "Your trip" on 10, the closing band on 1 and 5. |
| **The ticket stub is the signature** | Anything you hold (a trail day, a pick, a pass, a lane, a step) is a ticket: a dashed perforation and two notches punched in the canvas colour. Vertical perforations join the four steps on the For creators page into one long ticket. The **stamp** lands on what's done: "LIVE" on a trail, "BOOKED" on a pass. |
| **The trail is a ticket line** | A shared trail uses the cockpit's ticket line: one stub per day, with the creator's photo where the cockpit shows its live sky. The same stub appears in the drafting screen (built one at a time), the editor (day rail), the For creators preview and the trail page. |
| **Chrome** | Desktop: the cockpit's top islands. Brand on the left, then a nav pill whose tabs name the space you're in, then the Beacon or the main action on the right. The spaces are: **Public** (Trails · For creators · Plan a trip), **Creator** (Trails · Your trails · Stats), **Trip** (Trip · Book · Family · Ask, exactly as the cockpit), **Admin** (Creators · Trails · Reports). Phone: the floating opaque tab bar with a round ink centre button: Ask in a trip, **Leave a trail** (+) in the public and creator spaces. Full-screen tools (pick, drafting, editor, claim, Where to?) drop the tab bar for a back or close button and a bottom **dock** that holds the one action. |
| **Type** | Geist. Desktop body 16px; phone body 17px, secondary 16px, nothing below 16 except all-caps labels (13px) and fine print (14px), as in the cockpit. Public pages may be loud: the hero is two-tone (ink, then grey continuation) at 76px desktop and 46px phone. The app screens stay at the cockpit's scale. |
| **Targets** | At least 48px on the phone: buttons 48 (primary 56), chips 40 in a 48 row, Change or cancel 48, age pickers 48, steppers 48, tabs 56 in a 68px bar. |
| **Honest money** | Every lane says who earns, in words: "Juno's link: if you book, Aviasales pays Juno, not us", "No commission", "Partner link", "Buy direct". No invented prices. A total only ever comes "from your confirmation". Creator stats count clicks and never guess earnings. |
| **Motion** | Phone, Liquid: whatever opens grows out of what you tapped (520ms spring, k170 c26), stubs rise in 60ms apart, the stamp drops (640ms, k260 c16), presses squash to 0.96 on the k380 c30 spring. Desktop, calm: the same calls become a 180ms fade, with no zoom, spring or flight. Live numbers (the Thank count, drafting progress, the total) cross-fade in place in 140ms and never bounce. Reduced motion: 150ms fades only, and the drafting sheen stops. These are the cockpit's `GA.motion.open/close/settle` and `calm()` rules, unchanged. |

## The screens

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **For creators** (`creators`) | The public pitch | The paste box comes first, under "Take a trip. / Leave a trail.", with its kinds (a blog post, a whole blog, YouTube, an Instagram caption) and one **Leave a trail** button. ## Rules check
- **Fit:** the app screens at 1440×900 fit without scrolling (pick, drafting, editor, stats, claim, admin, where, detail). Public pages (For creators, trail, profile) scroll by design; their full length is in `-full.png`.
- **Texture:** opaque surfaces only. The Thank button, kind tiles and passes use the cockpit's tint and ink pairs, so contrast matches the cockpit's checked table (white on kind fills is 5:1 or more; tinted ink is 9:1 or more).
- **Not yet checked:** 1280×800, real photo crops (the renders stand in), and the Scrapbook theme with portrait photos.
