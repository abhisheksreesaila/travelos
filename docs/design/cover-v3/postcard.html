GitAway · one trip, passed along

Every trip starts as a wish.

Say it the way you'd tell a friend. GitAway turns one sentence into every way to go.

Start a tripFollow the postcard
1Compare

Every way to go, on one card.

Places down, days across. Each square is the whole trip for four: flights, stays, one total. The best one lights up.

2Book

Tap a square. That's the trip.

The flight, both stays and the sky, in one view. Book it in one go.

3Plan

Everyone gets a pen.

Your people add the zoo, the tacos, the sled hill to one calendar. A creator's guide adds the tip that saves a morning.

4Go

Then you just go.

Beach on day one, snow by day four. The plan, the weather and the bookings ride along.

5Share

Bring it home as a guide.

Your days, your tips, in your own words. Nothing invented; prices and bookings stay private.

6Post

Give it to the world.

One card for Instagram, one link back to the whole trip.

7Pass it on

And someone else's trip begins.

The Riveras in Austin made it theirs in one tap. Give back, get back, and round it goes.
