F-239 · The opened square and the checkout three options each, phone and laptop

Click a square, see the whole trip, then book it in one short flow.

The founder’s words: “I look at the hotel, I click on the flight I’ve chosen, I see the weather for those four days … in the same view; then we book it.” Every frame is live: switch the room, the flight and the photos; walk each checkout to its confirmation. San Diego, Fri 12 to Tue 16 Dec, 2 adults and kids 7 and 10, from SFO. Sample prices; the hotel’s photos are drawn stand-ins for LiteAPI’s; the sky is typical for those days, not a forecast.
