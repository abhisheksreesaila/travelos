    Why from above

    Hotel photos are framed to flatter. From a few hundred metres up you see what the listing leaves out: the freeway, the runway, the parking lot, how far the water really is.

    Truth pins come from OpenStreetMap, measured from the hotel's door as the crow flies, with walking and driving time estimated from that distance.

    Photoreal 3D needs a Google key

    This view streams Google's Photorealistic 3D Tiles through the Map Tiles API, drawn by CesiumJS.

    • It is metered: Google bills per root tileset request (one per session here), plus the tiles that follow under its usage limits.
    • Cesium ion offers Google's tiles free, but only for non-commercial use, so a commercial GitAway would use its own Google key.
    • It is heavier than the light view: textured meshes, more memory, more data. On a phone the detail is turned down and the tile cache capped.

    What you would see: the hotel as a real textured 3D model, the cars in the lot, the planes on the apron, the tree line on the shore.

    Kept only in this browser's localStorage. It never leaves your machine except to Google.
