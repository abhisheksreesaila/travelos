# Credits

## Code and techniques
- **three.js r187** (MIT, © 2010–2025 three.js authors). A tree-shaken copy is bundled into `dist/a.js`, `dist/b.js` and `dist/c.js` by `tools/build.mjs` from the npm package `three@0.187.0`; the licence header stays inline in each bundle.
- **Meng To's agent-skills** (MIT, © Meng To, cloned to `~/.cache/mengto-skills-src`, outside the repo). Nothing is copied wholesale; these ideas and recipes were adapted, with the file each lives in:
  - `web-design/build-threejs-scroll-worlds` (the primary skill, its Kage demo and references): one persistent world and one reversible scroll state; native scroll as the only source of truth, with an exact value for the DOM and a damped one (rate 5.2) for the camera (`references/scroll-conductor.js`, adapted in `src/page.js`); chapter endpoints composed per layout before interpolating, position and target on Catmull–Rom curves, field of view per segment (`src/scene.js` `setRig`, `placeCamera`); the quality governor that lowers the pixel ratio from a rolling frame-time window before giving up on the world, with Kage's 23 ms and 13.8 ms thresholds (`src/scene.js` `govern`); pause when hidden, the `dt` clamp, `?shot=` deterministic states, a poster plus chapter stills as the fallback, DOM text over a fixed canvas, in-world labels mirrored as real DOM; particles recycled ahead of the camera (Kage's leaf fall: `src/fx.js` `buildSnow`, `buildLeaves`); procedural canvas textures instead of image files (the guidebook pages and map labels, `src/book.js`, `src/world.js` `labelCanvas`).
  - `web-design/scroll-world-storytelling`: choosing the Three.js mode because the metaphor is a place, the beat ledger (hook, old way, new rule, mechanism, proof, payoff, action) and the style bible in the README, and "reduced motion uses the poster or ordered stills".
  - `web-design/cinematic-scroll-storytelling`: pinned story cards and the pacing vocabulary. Its stack (GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis) is deliberately not used: the primary skill keeps native scroll, and leaving them out saves about 50 KB.
  - `web-design/threejs-landscape`: the analytic height function kept apart from every mesh; domain-warped fBm plus ridged noise; ground coloured by slope, height and moisture with no textures; resolution that follows the lens (its polar grid became a foveated grid here); stars confined to the visible band in three size classes scaled by DPR; time of day as interpolated full states. The 2-D simplex noise in `src/core.js` follows its demo.
  - `3d/3d-four-seasons`: one recognisable place in four seasons from one shared state: the snow line, snow settling where the ground is high and flat, evergreens keeping their needles while aspen crowns thin to bare branches, seasonal light and sky, blends that redirect instead of queueing (`src/concept-b.js`).
  - `3d/3d-sky-background`: one sky function shared by the dome, the lake's reflections and the haze, so water meets sky without a line; one sun direction for the disc, the glow and the light; continuous day, dusk and night weights (`src/world.js` `SKY_GLSL`).
  - `3d/3d-ultra-realistic-water`: per-pixel normals from the analytic derivatives of a small wave sum, each wave faded at its own pixel footprint; Schlick Fresnel with water's F0 of 0.02; the reflection vector clamped above the horizon; the glitter terms; haze into the sky's horizon colour (`src/world.js` `buildWater`). Not used: Gerstner displacement, planar reflections, wakes and foam (it's a calm lake).
  - `3d/3d-falling-leaves`: instanced leaves whose sideways slip follows their tumble, `side = −(slip/ω)·cos(angle)`, with persistent seeded values and recycling ahead of the camera (`src/fx.js` `buildLeaves`).
  - `3d/3d-sky-rays`: read, and left out. A screen-space ray pass is a full-screen cost a mid phone can't spare; golden hour's glow lives in the sky function instead.
  - `game-development/build-mobile-threejs-games` and `optimize-threejs-games`: the phone as a primary surface (a lighter tier by default, a capped pixel ratio, controls in the thumb zone, URL-bar resizes ignored), and measuring on one repeatable path (the scripted scroll in `tools/measure.mjs`) with draw calls and triangles recorded.
- **The story3d prototype** in this repo (`docs/design/story3d/`): the render-loop lifecycle (itself adapted from Meng To's ThreeUI Gallery, MIT), springs, the vertex-colour merge (`Kit`) and the round-headed family figures.
