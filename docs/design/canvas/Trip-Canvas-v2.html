PLAN STEPS V2 · THE TRIP CANVAS

From a long text to a trip you can move with your thumb

Today the plan lives in Messages: one long text, no days, the same list twice, and notes you have to match up in your head. Here you paste it or say it, tap Convert, answer two quick questions, and it becomes colourful blocks on one trip canvas you can zoom from the week to a day to a single ride. Each note sits on the block it's about. Anything you don't want goes to a Set aside tray, still in the trip. The rides and notes are the family's own words; the arrival and home flights and the lunch places in Ask are samples.

What we'd build first

In order. Each one is useful on its own.

  • 1
    Paste and ConvertPaste the family's messages exactly as they are, or say them, and get park days, areas and rides with the notes kept on them. It asks who the initials are and which day each park is, and saves nothing until Add to trip.
  • 2
    The trip canvasOne zoomable view, from the week to a day to a ride, replacing the separate trip and calendar pages. Notes live on their blocks, and a Set aside tray keeps the maybes.
  • 3
    Touch moves and filtersHold and drag a block to a new time, another day or the tray, with Undo. Filter by who (everyone, adults, kids) and by lists like Pregnancy-safe.
  • 4
    Speak to itSay a change and see it as before and after; Apply makes it and tells the family what changed.
  • 5
    Ask, with searchAsk for ideas and the assistant searches, then offers cards you can add with a tap. This uses the Azure model, which is now set up.
