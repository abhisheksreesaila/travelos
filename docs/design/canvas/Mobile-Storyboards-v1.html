What the assistant needs

The services behind "Ask", "Around you" and the leave-by times.

  • An AI keyClaude, or OpenAI or Gemini (you have some of these). It understands what you asked and drafts the new plan.
    Key needed
  • Google PlacesNearby search, phone numbers, opening hours, ratings and the vegetarian filter.
    Key needed
  • Google DirectionsDrive times for "leave by". Apple Maps links can open directions with no key.
    Key needed for leave-by
  • Uber linksOpens Uber with the destination filled in.
    No key
  • Web pushTells the family when the plan changes. No email service needed.
    No key

Your location is used only while the app is open, and only sent for the search you asked for. It isn't stored or tracked.
